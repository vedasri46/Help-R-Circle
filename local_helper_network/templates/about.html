Our story, mission, and the team behind Help R Circle.

Our Mission

Bridging the Gap in Community Support

Every day, thousands of elderly citizens, people recovering from injuries, pregnant women, and individuals living alone struggle with simple tasks like buying groceries, collecting medicines, or paying utility bills.

Help R Circle was built to bridge this gap. We connect people who need everyday assistance with caring, verified volunteers from their own neighbourhood — creating a network of trust, compassion, and community resilience.

Our platform is completely free, easy to use, and designed with elderly users in mind — large text, simple navigation, and no complicated steps.

Our Solution
